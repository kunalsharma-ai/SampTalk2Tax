## Working differentiation hypotheses (to be tested by the research, not claims)

1. **Ledger integrity you can prove:** database-enforced invariants and a verifiable hash-chained audit trail exposed to users and auditors.
2. **Source-backed AI:** every AI answer carries source records, period, method, assumptions and confidence; AI never posts or pays without approval.
3. **One product across GL, tax (India-first), payroll, spend and treasury** for SMBs at SMB pricing, with enterprise extensibility.
4. **Explainable automation:** an AI action ledger and agent control center with per-agent permissions and a kill switch.
