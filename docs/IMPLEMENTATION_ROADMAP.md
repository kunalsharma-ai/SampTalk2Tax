# Implementation roadmap

The directive (Part 47) defines milestones M0–M9. This file tracks what each one contains and where it stands. Feature-level status is in [`FEATURE_STATUS.md`](FEATURE_STATUS.md).

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **M0 Discovery & architecture** | PRD, competitive matrix, domain model, ADRs, DB design, API contracts, security, AI architecture, design system spec, roadmap, dependency map | **Partly done.** Done: PRD, feature registry (1,343 entries), architecture + ADRs, database schema, accounting engine spec, security, API docs, test strategy, deployment, roadmap. Not done: competitive matrix research (framework only), AI architecture, design system spec, tax engine, workflow engine, integration architecture, dashboard specs, pricing |
| **M1 Functional foundation** | Monorepo, auth, tenants, onboarding, RBAC, database, accounting engine, GL, CoA, journal posting, basic reports, initial dashboard | **Built.** Backend and web app are built and tested (unit, integration and browser tests). Hardening so far: server-side sessions with rotating refresh tokens, revocation and a Security page; sign-in lockout and rate limiting; PostgreSQL row-level security; member invitations, role changes and removal. M1 is complete |
| **M2 Core finance** | AR, AP, banking, reconciliation, expenses, customers, vendors, documents, approval workflows | **In progress.** Slice 1 built and tested: customers, vendors, tax rates, invoices, credit notes, bills, vendor credits, receipts, payments, allocation, statements, ageing and slice 2 built and tested: bank accounts, CSV statement import, matching, bank entries and receipts from statement lines, reconciliation with history and undo; slice 3 built and tested: attachments on records and single-step approval rules for sales and purchase documents; slice 4 built and tested: employee expense claims with receipts, approval and reimbursement (see [`MILESTONE_REPORT_M2.md`](MILESTONE_REPORT_M2.md)). The M2 scope in the directive is covered at a first level; remaining depth is listed in KNOWN_LIMITATIONS |
| M3 Tax & payroll | Validated India GST/TDS workflows, payroll finance, compliance calendars | **Built (India).** Report: [`MILESTONE_REPORT_M3.md`](MILESTONE_REPORT_M3.md). Slice 1 built and tested: India GST (registration, GSTIN check, GST rates with categories, place of supply, CGST/SGST/UTGST/IGST split and posting, HSN/SAC, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B working papers with ITC set-off and a ledger check); see [`TAX_ENGINE_INDIA_GST.md`](TAX_ENGINE_INDIA_GST.md). Slice 2 built and tested: TDS/TCS (Income-tax Act 2025 sections, PAN, thresholds, preview on bills and invoices, quarterly register, challans) and GST reverse charge including import of services; see [`TAX_ENGINE_INDIA_TDS_TCS.md`](TAX_ENGINE_INDIA_TDS_TCS.md). Slice 3 built and tested: India payroll (employees, dated salary structures, monthly pay runs with PF/EPS/EDLI, ESI, Maharashtra professional tax and new-regime TDS on salary, ledger posting, statutory payments with due dates, payslips); see [`PAYROLL_INDIA.md`](PAYROLL_INDIA.md). Slice 4 built and tested: compliance calendar (GST monthly/QRMP, TDS/TCS deposits and statements, payroll dues, advance tax; payments settled from records, returns marked filed); see [`COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR_INDIA.md`](COMPLIANCE_CALENDAR_INDIA.md). Slice 5 built and tested: e-invoicing (INV-01 JSON for the IRP, importing and checking the IRP response, QR code, 30-day limit, cancellation within 24 hours); see [`E_INVOICING_INDIA.md`](E_INVOICING_INDIA.md). Slice 6 built and tested: e-way bills (which invoices need one, the portal's bulk JSON with Part B, validity under Rule 138(10), vehicle changes, cancellation within 24 hours, no void with a live e-way bill); see [`E_WAY_BILLS_INDIA.md`](E_WAY_BILLS_INDIA.md). Not started: a direct IRP, GSP or e-way bill portal connection |
| M4 Financial intelligence | Forecasting, budgeting, analytics, AI copilot, alerts, leakage | **In progress.** Slice 1 built and tested: budgets (monthly amounts per revenue and expense account; versions started empty, copied or from last year’s actuals with a change; approval by someone other than the submitter; supersession) and budget vs actual by month, quarter or year; see [`BUDGETS.md`](BUDGETS.md). Slice 2 built and tested: cash-flow forecasting over 30 to 365 days from open invoices and bills at each contact’s usual payment delay plus planned items, with a low/high range, a minimum-balance warning, saved forecasts and backtests measured against actual cash; see [`CASH_FLOW_FORECAST.md`](CASH_FLOW_FORECAST.md). Slice 3 built and tested: 13 KPIs from posted journals with targets and history, revenue by customer, and a monthly management report (vs previous month, a year before and the approved budget) with commentary that freezes when finalised; see [`KPIS_AND_MANAGEMENT_REPORTS.md`](KPIS_AND_MANAGEMENT_REPORTS.md). Slice 4 built and tested: alerts from nine rule-based checks (cash below the minimum, overdue customers, filings due, KPI targets, budget overruns, regular customers not billed, duplicate bills and payments, unusual manual journals), each with its evidence, acknowledged, dismissed with a reason, cleared when no longer found and reopened when found again; see [`ALERTS_AND_LEAKAGE.md`](ALERTS_AND_LEAKAGE.md). Slice 5 built and tested without a live key: the AI copilot on Anthropic's Claude, answering questions from eleven read-only tools over these figures (reports, ageing, KPIs, management report, cash flow, budget vs actual, alerts, compliance), listing the reports each answer used, with saved private conversations, an organisation switch, a per-member limit and a clear "not set up" state when no key is set; see [`AI_COPILOT.md`](AI_COPILOT.md). Waiting on `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` for a live check |
| M5 Autonomous finance | Agent framework, control center, workflow automation, AI reconciliation/collections/close, explainable AI log | **In progress.** Slice 1 built and tested: workflow automations (eight triggers read from the audit trail as a transactional outbox, conditions on each event's facts, tasks and signed webhooks with retries, dry-run testing, immutable versions, run log, retry by hand) and a task queue for people and automations; see [`WORKFLOW_AUTOMATIONS.md`](WORKFLOW_AUTOMATIONS.md). Slice 2 built and tested: schedule triggers (daily, weekly or monthly at a time in India time; an invoice N days overdue; a bill due in N days), each acting once per occurrence and never on a backlog, and email actions over SMTP to a role, a member or the customer or vendor, with retries; email is waiting on a real SMTP server for a live check. Slice 3 built and tested without a live key: an AI collections agent that drafts one payment reminder per overdue customer from the books with a risk level and reasons, checks every amount and invoice number against the books, and sends only what a person approves (with edits, re-checked against current balances), plus an AI activity log of every AI call with model, tokens, outcome and decision; see [`AI_COLLECTIONS.md`](AI_COLLECTIONS.md). Slice 4 built and tested without a live key: AI bank reconciliation, suggesting for each unmatched bank line a match to ledger lines, a receipt or payment against open invoices or bills, or an entry, with confidence and reason, checked against what it was shown and applied only when a person accepts it, through the same code as by hand; see [`AI_RECONCILIATION.md`](AI_RECONCILIATION.md). Slice 5 built and tested without a live key: month-end close, with a checklist worked out from the books, tasks for open checks, an AI review that proposes adjusting entries (accruals reversing next month and others) checked and posted only when a person approves them, and closing that locks the month with a certification of the figures, plus reopening with a reason; see [`MONTH_END_CLOSE.md`](MONTH_END_CLOSE.md). Slice 6 built and tested: an AI control center showing every agent's action level, switch, monthly token budget, usage, failures and acceptance rate, with an organisation-wide emergency stop that also halts work already running; see [`AI_CONTROL_CENTER.md`](AI_CONTROL_CENTER.md). Not built: branches, SMS or chat, money cost of AI, an orchestrator between agents |
| M6 Enterprise finance | Multi-entity, consolidation, treasury, fixed assets, advanced reporting, governance | **In progress.** Slice 1 built and tested: a fixed asset register (purchase posted or already in the books, or brought in part-depreciated), straight-line and written-down-value depreciation posted monthly as one journal with catch-up and latest-first reversal, and disposal with gain or loss; see [`FIXED_ASSETS.md`](FIXED_ASSETS.md). Slice 2 built and tested: prepaid expense and deferred revenue schedules released evenly or by days in one monthly journal with catch-up, ending early with a refund, and month-end accruals with automatic reversal; see [`PREPAID_AND_DEFERRED.md`](PREPAID_AND_DEFERRED.md). Slice 3 built and tested: departments (cost centres) and projects as tags on journal and document lines, checked by kind and active state, kept on reversals, with profit and loss by department or project and a filtered profit and loss; see [`FINANCIAL_DIMENSIONS.md`](FINANCIAL_DIMENSIONS.md). Slice 4 built and tested: loans with EMI or equal-principal schedules, installments split into principal and interest, prepayments, floating-rate changes and latest-first reversal, and a treasury overview of cash, borrowings, installments due and the ledger check; see [`LOANS_AND_TREASURY.md`](LOANS_AND_TREASURY.md). Slice 5 built and tested: invoice templates from eight country presets with five themes, palettes and custom colours, editable and reorderable sections and columns, logos and safe HTML templates, printing or saving as PDF, and e-signatures on posted invoices with a recorded content hash; see [`INVOICE_TEMPLATES.md`](INVOICE_TEMPLATES.md). Slice 6 built and tested: CSV, Excel and PDF exports of the trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, general ledger, receivables and payables ageing and profit by department or project, audited with the file hash; see [`REPORT_EXPORTS.md`](REPORT_EXPORTS.md). Slice 7 built and tested: consolidation groups of companies under a parent, with currency translation, elimination rules and a consolidated worksheet, profit and loss and balance sheet; see [`CONSOLIDATION.md`](CONSOLIDATION.md). Slice 8 built and tested: ownership percentages with non-controlling interests shown within equity and in the profit split. Slice 9 built and tested: intercompany charges and settlements posted in both companies at once, reversed together and eliminated by their exact amounts. |

Slice 2: the scenario war room (impact of each change by department, measured suggestions, versions, notes, approval, planning board, risk map); see [`WAR_ROOM.md`](WAR_ROOM.md). Slice 5 built and tested: privacy-preserving benchmarking (ten metrics from the books, opt-in with consent, a pseudonymous pool no tenant can read, medians from 5 and quartiles from 10 other participants, synthetic peers for demo organisations only).
